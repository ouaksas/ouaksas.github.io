Privacy Policy

Cozy Escape Room · Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cozy Escape Room app ("the app") uses. The app is made by Omar Aitouakas ("we").

No account, game data stays on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account. Your progress (rooms, inventory, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. No ad is shown while you play a room. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and information about ad interactions.

  • iOS: the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • Europe, UK and US states with privacy laws: a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) lets you choose. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • Ads are set to a "G" (general audiences) content rating. If your age is unknown or under 13, only non-personalized ads are requested.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your daily gift is ready). No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The app is family friendly and made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads requested for users whose age is unknown or under 13 are non-personalized.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the app's Settings › Privacy.
  • Limit tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: omar.aitouaksas@gmail.com.
